Our Story

MOME was born from a simple idea: clothing should feel good, last long, and leave a lighter footprint. We design pieces you'll reach for every day — not trends that fade by season.

Every garment is made with responsibly sourced materials and produced in small batches to reduce waste. Quality over quantity, always.
